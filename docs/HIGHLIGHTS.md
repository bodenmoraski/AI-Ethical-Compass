## 💎 Top 5 Unique Differentiators

### 1. **AI as Teaching Assistant, Not Just Content Generator** ⭐⭐⭐

**What Makes It Unique**: We're not using AI to generate content—we're using GPT-4 to *analyze ethical reasoning* and provide personalized feedback at scale.

**The Innovation**:
- **Triple-Layer AI Moderation System**:
  - Appropriateness detection
  - Topical relevance verification (requires 2+ scenario-specific references)
  - Quality scoring (0.0-1.0)
  
- **Ethical Framework Detection**: AI automatically identifies which ethical theories students are using (utilitarianism, deontology, virtue ethics, care ethics)

- **Bias Scoring**: Measures one-sided reasoning (0.0-1.0 scale)

- **Constructive Feedback Generation**: Personalized improvement suggestions for every submission

**Why It Matters**: This solves the "impossible problem" of providing personalized feedback on ethical reasoning at scale—something that previously required one-on-one time with an ethics professor.

**Sound Bite**: *"We've turned AI from a writing tool into a Socratic teaching assistant that helps students develop critical thinking skills."*

### 2. **Real-Time Classroom Intelligence** ⭐⭐⭐

**What Makes It Unique**: First educational ethics platform with live, WebSocket-based classroom monitoring providing teachers with unprecedented visibility.

**The Innovation**:
- **Live Activity Feed**: Real-time student actions without page refresh
- **Automatic At-Risk Identification**: Algorithm identifies struggling students instantly
- **Engagement Scoring**: Calculated in real-time (0.0-1.0) per student
- **Session Tracking**: Detailed action logs (JSONB stored) showing what students are doing

**Why It Matters**: Teachers get insights in real-time, enabling immediate intervention instead of discovering problems weeks later during grading.

**Sound Bite**: *"It's like having X-ray vision into student learning—seeing engagement patterns, identifying struggles, and intervening before students fall behind."*
