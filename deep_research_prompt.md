# Simplified Deep Research Prompt for AI Ethics Educational Resources

## Research Objective
Find and compile a comprehensive list of high-quality educational resources focused on AI ethics, responsible AI use, and ethical AI implementation in education. Focus on practical, accessible resources that educators and students can use immediately.

## What We Need
A simple list of resources with:
- **Name/Title**
- **Direct Link** 
- **Brief Description** (1-2 sentences)
- **Tags** (3-5 relevant keywords)

## Resource Categories to Find

### 1. Guidelines & Policies
- Official AI ethics guidelines from educational organizations
- Policy documents and frameworks
- Standards and best practices

### 2. Research & Studies  
- Academic papers on AI ethics in education
- Research reports and white papers
- Empirical studies and findings

### 3. Tools & Platforms
- AI ethics assessment tools
- Bias detection software
- Educational AI platforms
- Interactive learning tools

### 4. Courses & Tutorials
- Online courses about AI ethics
- Workshop materials and guides
- Training programs for educators
- Student learning modules

### 5. Case Studies
- Real-world examples of AI in education
- Implementation stories and lessons learned
- Success and failure case studies

### 6. Ethical Frameworks
- Decision-making models for AI ethics
- Assessment rubrics and checklists
- Evaluation frameworks

### 7. Communities & Networks
- Professional organizations
- Online forums and discussion groups
- Collaborative platforms
- Teacher networks

### 8. Assessment Tools
- Rubrics for evaluating AI use
- Student assessment frameworks
- Evaluation tools and surveys

## Sources to Search

### Academic & Research
- Google Scholar, JSTOR, IEEE Xplore
- Education databases (ERIC, Education Research Complete)
- AI/ML conferences and journals

### Government & Organizations
- UNESCO, OECD, World Economic Forum
- US Department of Education, European Commission
- ISTE, ASCD, AERA, SITE
- National educational ministries

### Technology Companies
- Google AI, Microsoft Education, IBM Research
- OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepMind
- Mozilla Foundation, Electronic Frontier Foundation

### Universities
- MIT, Stanford, Harvard, Carnegie Mellon
- International universities with AI ethics programs
- Educational research centers

## Quality Criteria
- **Relevant**: Directly about AI ethics in education
- **Accessible**: Free or affordable, available in English
- **Current**: Published within last 5 years (with exceptions for important older works)
- **Practical**: Something educators or students can actually use
- **Credible**: From reputable organizations or experts

## Output Format

Provide results in this simple JSON format:

```json
{
  "resources": [
    {
      "title": "Resource Name",
      "url": "https://direct-link-to-resource.com",
      "description": "Brief 1-2 sentence description of what this resource is and how it helps with AI ethics education.",
      "tags": ["tag1", "tag2", "tag3", "tag4"],
      "category": "guidelines|research|tools|courses|case-studies|frameworks|communities|assessment"
    }
  ]
}
```

## Focus Areas
- **K-12 Education**: Resources specifically for high school and middle school
- **Teacher Training**: Materials for educators to learn about AI ethics
- **Student Learning**: Resources students can use directly
- **Practical Implementation**: Tools and guides that can be used in classrooms
- **Global Perspective**: Resources from different countries and cultures

## Success Metrics
- **Quantity**: Aim for 100+ high-quality resources
- **Coverage**: Resources across all 8 categories
- **Diversity**: Mix of organizations, countries, and approaches
- **Quality**: All resources should be immediately useful

## Timeline
- **1-2 days** for comprehensive research
- Focus on finding the best resources, not deep analysis
- Prioritize quantity and variety over detailed analysis

**Goal**: A comprehensive, diverse list of practical AI ethics resources that educators and students can immediately use.
